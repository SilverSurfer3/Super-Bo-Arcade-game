How to play

The Rules

Take the handoff and run a booby-trapped field. When a defender gets his hands on Bo, it turns into a street fight.

Gold coins: 3 points each Lightning makes Bo bigger, faster and unstoppable Blue lightning: one per game. The field parts and Bo gets a clear road Mines and spikes: hurdle them Crosses and posts: go around. The Enforcer, the one purple jersey in the game, starts a street fight. He lines up every play until you beat him. Anyone else tackles you, unless you press Z to pick a fight first
Downs: 4 tries to gain 10 yards. Fail and the game is over.
Scoring: ½ per yard · 3 per coin · 2 per defender beaten · +5 bonus for every knockout
Run: ←→ steer · Space hurdle · A spin · W flip · D bat · Z pick a fight
Fight: ←→ move · ↑ jump · ↓ block · A karate kick · D ball & chain · S katana · W home run (foe under 25%) · P pause
